Who we are

This policy explains what personal information OmniBioAI collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It covers this website, the hosted platform, OmniBioAI API, OmniBioAI Studio, and our documentation.

“OmniBioAI”, “we”, and “us” mean the operator of these services, based in Kansas City, USA. Contact manish@omnibioai.org with privacy questions or requests.

The short version

  • Studio is local-first. Research data run on your own machine stays on your infrastructure.
  • We collect account details, content sent to hosted services, billing usage, and security logs needed to operate the service.
  • Stripe handles card details; we do not see full card numbers.
  • We do not sell personal information or use customer content to train AI models.
  • Do not send PHI to hosted services unless we have signed a business associate agreement.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Beta requests: name, institutional email, organization, role, research area, operating system, and intended analysis.
  • Account information: email, name, salted password hash, organization, memberships, and roles. Federated sign-in provides basic profile data and a provider identifier.
  • Billing: billing contact, address, and tax ID if provided. Stripe stores card data; we receive a customer reference and limited card details.
  • Hosted content: questions, uploaded files, workflow parameters, chat, and comments.
  • Support: emails, bug reports, feedback, and community messages.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage records needed for billing and plan limits.
  • Security and audit logs including sign-ins, permission decisions, IP address, user agent, and request identifiers.
  • Cryptographic API-key hashes and short prefixes; not the full key after display.
  • Error diagnostics and request metadata.
  • Desktop licence checks containing the key and basic technical information, not research data.
  • Standard website request data received by the host and font provider. This site does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use information

We use information to provide and operate services, review beta requests, bill usage, secure accounts, detect abuse, fix errors, answer support requests, send service notices, and comply with law. Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the bases are contract performance, steps requested before a contract, legitimate interests, and legal obligations as appropriate.

We do not use your content to train machine-learning models, sell or rent personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with contracted processors or where you direct us: Stripe; hosting providers including Cloudflare and GitHub Pages; selected identity providers; error tracking and email providers; AI model providers you explicitly enable; and your organization’s administrators.

We may disclose information to comply with valid legal process, protect users and services, or as part of a corporate transaction where this policy continues to apply.

Health and research data

OmniBioAI is built with HIPAA-aligned security controls, but this is not a certification. Hosted services must not receive PHI unless we have signed a BAA with you.

When Studio runs on your hardware, PHI and research data are processed in your environment and you remain responsible for compliance. External services are used only when enabled, and you remain responsible for data you choose to send.

How long we keep it

  • Account information: while active, then deleted or anonymized within 90 days unless law requires longer.
  • Hosted content: until you delete it or close the account.
  • Usage and billing records: as needed for billing and typically seven years for accounting.
  • Security logs: up to six years, or longer under legal hold.
  • Beta requests: up to two years unless you become a customer.

How we protect it

Controls include encryption in transit, hashed passwords and API keys, short-lived access tokens, organization-scoped roles, MFA options, least-privilege service credentials, and append-only security audit records. No system is perfectly secure; affected users will be notified as required by law.

Your rights and choices

Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, object to, or restrict processing and to withdraw consent. California residents may know, delete, and correct information without discrimination; we do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.

Contact manish@omnibioai.org. We verify identity and respond within legally required timeframes, generally 30–45 days. EEA and UK residents may complain to their data-protection authority. Organization-provided accounts may be controlled by that organization.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and providers may process information elsewhere. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as European Commission standard contractual clauses.

Children

Services are intended for researchers and professionals, not children under 16. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was collected so it can be deleted.

Cookies and local storage

Only storage needed to operate services is used, including sign-in, preferences, and abuse prevention. There are no advertising cookies. Blocking essential storage may prevent the hosted platform from working.

Changes to this policy

Updates will appear here with a new date. Material changes will be communicated to account holders by email or in-product before taking effect. Questions: manish@omnibioai.org.
